# fw-expansion-port-detail-stickers
A simple set of stickers show the details of each USB port for various Framework laptop mainboards. The aim here was to create small stickers which quickly show the port specifications to be printed off and applied in the expansion slots to inform you at a glance which expansion cards are suitable per port. This is more of a concern with the AMD boards specifically, since on the AMD boards it can vary per port.

# Port Numbering
As for the numbering of the ports, the rule of thumb is back right to front right, then back left to front left, while the laptop is face down and hinge away from you (the most likely position it will be in while you are swapping expansion cards). I kept it consistent with the way Framework numbers them in their diagrams, linked below.

# Sources
Info taken from the official FW port definition documents:

https://knowledgebase.frame.work/usb-port-definition-matrix-framework-laptop-13-pro-HJU7aVrpx

https://knowledgebase.frame.work/en_us/usb-port-definition-matrix-framework-laptop-13-HkHVUHaTge

https://knowledgebase.frame.work/en_us/usb-port-definition-matrix-framework-laptop-16-SkFD30Rlx


Consider the stickers to paraphrase / condense the most important info, but as always refer to the manufacturer for full specs, there are lots of nuances that cant be encapsulated into little stickers, details like "Ports 1,2,3, and 4 are shared on a first come first serve basis. So they are 1.5A by default, but one port from any of these can negotiate 3A over PD. Once that is taken, the other ports are limited to 1.5A.". Details like this that apply to all ports across most FW mainboards and aren't easy to show in a sticker got left out, so don't consider them a definitive source.
Feel free to reach out if you notice mistakes, but I did make efforts to triple check the details.


# Usage
Feel free to print them out and use them as you see fit. I don't plan to make printing templates or anything like that. I got a small batch printed out and could mail some of the extras out within the US if there's interest, I might ask for $10 or so to cover costs of printing, shipping, and my time. Mostly I just made these for my own use since I got tired of looking up port definitions for my AMD board all the time and wanted to make it all available for anyone else who wants to print them out themselves.
